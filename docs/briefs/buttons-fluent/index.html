Buttons in the Fluent look: before and after

Your choice from the study (2026-10-24): the “Proposed (Fluent style)” buttons. Photographed at 150% screen scale, the same sample before and after.

What changes

  • Corners are 4px on every control (buttons, fields, selects, comboboxes, navigation items), in every density. This and the lighter border fix the corner that looked cut.
  • The default button’s border is light gray (gray-300), one step darker on hover (gray-350).
  • Hover and pressed fills are the same light grays at every size.
  • Selected and toggled-on buttons are gray with dark text, not pale blue.
  • The pale halo around primary buttons is gone.
  • Disabled buttons have a faint border instead of a dark one.
  • A subtle button’s icon turns blue on hover. Red commands (Delete) keep a red icon.

Buttons and a field

The 4px corner reaches every control, not only buttons

Buttons share one corner setting with the other controls, so this change moves them too. Measured on the live components:

  • Changes from 6px to 4px: buttons, text fields, selects, comboboxes, navigation items (and every other control on the same setting).
  • Stays the same: the menu (4px) and its items (square, 0px). Their corners come from the menu, not from the control setting; the command bar step gives menu items their own 4px corners.
  • Compact density was already 4px, so nothing changes there.
