### What goes wrong

useCollectionView gives GroupPanel only "select" and "multi" fields, so dates, numbers and text cannot group.

### Suggested fix

Every column: text by value, numbers by ranges (or the field's own buckets), dates by Day/Week/Month/Year, booleans as two groups; groupable: false to opt out.
