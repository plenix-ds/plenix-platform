### Fixed
- Navigation group headings no longer end in "…" with no way to read them (owner, #502). A heading in a NavList group wraps to two evenly balanced lines, and only past two lines ends in "…", with the full text in a tooltip on hover and keyboard focus (a folding heading) or on hover (a static one). A one-line heading stays 32px high and a two-line heading is 40px. At 192px "PROJECT COMMAND CENTER" reads in full on two lines. Other CollapsibleSection headings keep their one line; `CollapsibleSection` has a new `wrap` prop for the same behavior. The accessible name is still the full text.
