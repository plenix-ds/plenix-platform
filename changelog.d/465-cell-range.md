### Added
- Cell range selection in DataGrid, an option that is off by default (#465, part 1 of 2): `<DataGrid cellSelection>` lets a person select a block of cells with the keyboard. Shift with an arrow extends the block from the cell that had focus; Shift with Home or End goes to the first or last column of the row (Home and End alone keep their meaning); Escape or a plain arrow ends the block; Enter edits the active cell and ends it. The block has the selected fill and a border on its outer edges only (logical, so mirrored in right to left), and its cells carry `aria-selected`. Ctrl or Command with C copies the block as tab-separated text that Excel and Google Sheets paste as rows and columns (a cell with a tab, a line break or a quote is quoted; group rows are skipped; one cell copies as before). A screen reader hears "N cells selected" once the keys pause (not once per key press) and "N cells copied". The block is kept by row id and column id, so it grows past the drawn rows in a long grid and copies whole. It is separate from the row checkboxes. `onCellSelectionChange` hears `{ from, to, cellCount, rowCount, columnCount, fields }` and `null` when the block ends; the adapter adds `getCellRange()`, `clearCellRange()` and `copyCellRange()`. Without the option nothing changes: the engine's differential probe (DOM, focus, scroll, fetches, render and mutation counts, left to right and right to left) is identical before and after, except the known timing of one remote-sort step. New tokens only name existing roles: `data-grid-range-background`, `data-grid-range-border-color`, `data-grid-range-border-width`. The English and Arabic catalogs add `dataGrid.cellsSelected` and `dataGrid.cellsCopied`. The design spec adds a Cell selection example. Part 2 adds the pointer (drag and Shift with click). Test: `tests/data-grid-cell-range-465.test.js` (written, not run).
